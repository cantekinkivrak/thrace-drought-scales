Every file under `data/` is a **derived product**: monthly district-level aggregates, standardized
indices, fitted diagnostics and summary tables computed by the authors. These derived products are

appropriate credit by citing:

## Underlying source records — not redistributed

The derived products above were computed from records supplied by third parties. **Those raw
records are not included in this archive and are not covered by the licence above.**

**Turkish State Meteorological Service (MGM)** — daily and monthly precipitation and temperature
for the seven stations. Supplied to the authors for research use; MGM retains all rights and
redistribution of the raw station series is not permitted. Requests: https://www.mgm.gov.tr

**Turkish Statistical Institute (TÜİK)** — district-level wheat and sunflower production and
area statistics. Requests: https://www.tuik.gov.tr

What is distributed here are aggregated statistics (monthly means, standardized anomalies,
correlation and rank summaries) at a level of aggregation that does not permit reconstruction of
the raw station series or the underlying survey returns.

districts, redistributed here for reproducibility of the zonal aggregation.
